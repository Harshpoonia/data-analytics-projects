# Superstore Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data from a Superstore dataset to uncover business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional trends.

The objective is to apply data analytics techniques to identify key business drivers and provide actionable recommendations that support data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset

The dataset contains:

* 9,994 transactions
* 21 features
* Customer information
* Product information
* Regional sales data
* Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit metrics

## Key Findings

* West region generated the highest sales and profit.
* Technology was the highest-performing category in terms of both revenue and profitability.
* Furniture generated strong sales but comparatively low profit.
* November recorded the highest sales revenue.
* December generated the highest profit.
* A small group of products and customers contributed significantly to overall revenue.
* Higher discount levels were strongly associated with lower profitability.
* Discount showed a strong negative relationship with profit margin.
